Privacy Policy and Child Safety Standards

Hive chat & play live (Android app) · Effective October 15, 2026 · Last updated October 15, 2026

Hive chat & play live is a social chat app. This page explains what we collect, how we protect it, how you can delete it, and the standards we enforce to keep children safe. By using Hive chat & play live you agree to this policy.

If you choose an avatar, the app reads only the photo you pick and crops it on your device before upload. We do not collect precise location, your contacts, or your browsing history. We do not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

2. How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit: TLS for all API and WebSocket traffic.
  • Passwords: hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12), never stored in plain text.
  • Access control: signed tokens (JWT) and limited internal database access.
  • Hosting: secure cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud) with regular updates and monitoring.

No system is completely immune, but we work to reduce the risk of breaches and will act promptly if one occurs.

3. Data retention and account deletion

  • Public rooms: the last 100 messages per room are kept; older messages are removed automatically.
  • Private messages: the last 30 messages per conversation are kept; older ones are removed.
  • Account data: after a verified deletion request, your username, messages, friends, avatar, bio, and feed are permanently erased within 30 days.
  • Safety records: if content was reported for child safety or other serious abuse, we may keep the report and related evidence for as long as the law requires or an investigation needs, even after account deletion.

Delete directly in the app: open the top right menu, tap Settings, then tap Delete account and data. You can also follow the steps on our account deletion page or email orangepenguinflies@gmail.com with your username. Logging in within 7 days of an email request cancels it.

4. Sharing with third parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only:

  • With service providers that run the app for us: Google Cloud (hosting), Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), and Google Play (purchases).
  • When required by law, court order, or a valid legal request, including reports of child exploitation to the authorities.
  • To protect the safety and integrity of Hive chat & play live and its users, and to enforce our Terms.

5. Child Safety Standards

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) Hive chat & play live prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child sexual exploitation, grooming, sexualization of minors, sextortion, and any content or behavior that endangers a child. This applies to every room, private message, profile, avatar, and bio on Hive chat & play live.

What is prohibited

  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to sexual or sexualized imagery or text involving anyone under 18.
  • Grooming, or trying to build a relationship with a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, or threatening or pressuring a minor for sexual content or money.
  • Trading, advertising, or soliciting child exploitation material, including through coded language.
  • Using the app to arrange meetings with minors for sexual purposes.

Age requirement

Hive chat & play live is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete the account and its data. Parents or guardians can request removal at any time using the contact below.

How users can report

  • In the app: open the top right menu and tap Report to report a user. You can also use the report option on a message or profile.
  • By email: write to orangepenguinflies@gmail.com with the subject "URGENT: Child Safety".

What we do when we receive a report or detect a violation

  • Trained moderators review child safety reports within 24 hours.
  • We remove the content and permanently ban the account. Offenders are not allowed to return.
  • We preserve relevant evidence and report apparent CSAM and child endangerment to the appropriate authorities, such as the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement, and we cooperate fully with their investigations.
  • We never ask users to send us suspected CSAM. If you see it, report it and do not share or forward it.

Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy. A designated person is responsible for child safety at Hive chat & play live and is available to discuss our CSAM prevention practices and compliance at the address below.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you can ask us to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct your username, bio, or avatar (you can do this in the app).
  • Delete your account and data yourself in the app under top right menu > Settings > Delete account and data (see section 3).
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.

Email orangepenguinflies@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We reply within 30 days.

7. Local storage

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking identifiers. The app stores a secure sign-in token on your device to keep you logged in. Clearing the app's data in your device settings signs you out.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will tell you with an in-app notice and, if you gave us an email, by email. The latest version is always on this page. Continuing to use Hive chat & play live after a change means you accept the updated policy.
