Delete your Ather Hive account

This page is for the Ather Hive Android app (com.ather.hive). Delete it yourself in the app in a few taps, or send us an email. Either way, your account and data are permanently erased.

This cannot be undone

When deletion is complete, these are permanently erased:

  • Your username and profile (avatar and bio)
  • Your public and private messages
  • Your friends and connections
  • Your feed, likes, and reactions
  • Your push notification token and device identifier

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Ather Hive and tap the menu button at the top right.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

Your data is erased within 30 days.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Use this if you cannot open the app or no longer have it installed.

  1. Open your email app and write to orangepenguinflies@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Account Deletion Request - Ather Hive
  3. In the message, include your username and the email linked to your account (if you added one) so we can verify it is you.
  4. Send it. We reply to confirm we received your request within 72 hours.
  5. We verify your request, erase your data within 30 days, and email you when it is done.
